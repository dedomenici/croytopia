    About

    CROYTOPIA is a new kind of video repository for the collection, dissemination and analysis of site-specific community-generated ephemeral social history, and a unique and self-sustaining resource for measuring the effects and influences of the built environment on the communities that inhabit it.

    Participants are be invited to recount important and interesting personal facts and memories, triggered by locations in Croydon. These short videos are uploaded to an online map, and as the number of contributions increases, previously unseen patterns will hopefully begin to emerge, which could influence future local decision making and improvements to the public realm.

    The cumulative effect of lots of small changes can be profound, and CROYTOPIA has the potential to rebalance power in the favour of those whose voices may be overlooked, elevating the personal to the transcendent.

    If successful the concept could be scaled to benefit communities around the world.

    Documentation of CROYTOPIA will be exhibited at Turf Projects in Croydon in November 2023.

    CROYTOPIA is inspired by Richard’s work with collaborator Jess Mabel Jones, who together have wanted to make a project about Croydon for many years. Their first Croydon collaboration was American Assassin: Redux in 2018, and a prototype for CROYTOPIA was conducted in Camden in 2021.

    RICHARD DEDOMENICI

    is an artist, filmmaker and broadcaster from Watford who specialises in urban-absurdist interventions that strive to create the kind of uncertainty that leads to possibility. Richard has performed in over 30 countries, and is currently working on a self-guided walking tour of movie locations in the City of London.

    JESS MABEL JONES

    makes work that is good fun, good-looking and that strives to incite change. her work is eclectic, visually dynamic and a little bit rowdy. Jess is neurodivergent, a creative collaborator to other neurodivergent artists, and an accessible performance advocate. She is a Total Theatre Award winner and assessor and is born and bred in Thornton Heath, Croydon.
